What became of the fish

Every fish handled at a rack ends up somewhere: spawned for the next generation, passed above the rack to spawn naturally, surplussed, or dead before it could spawn.

Pre-spawn mortality

Eggs per spawner

Is the trend real?

Mann-Kendall is a rank-based test, so it does not assume a straight line or normal errors — appropriate for run data. Sen's slope estimates the size of the change.

Against the recent normal

Each season as a percentage difference from the preceding ten-season mean — the framing used to judge whether a run is unusual.

Are the runs shifting earlier or later?

The date by which half the season's fish had arrived, with a fitted trend. Measured from 1 March so runs crossing the new year stay comparable.

Forecasting next season from jacks

Jacks return a year early, so their number is a leading indicator of the adult run behind them.

Ocean conditions and what came back

Marine survival, not freshwater production, drives most of the variation in these runs. The PDO and NPGO summarise the state of the North Pacific a salmon entered as a smolt.

Correlation at every lag, not just the flattering one

Each cell is the correlation between a season's return and the ocean index that many years earlier. The whole grid is shown because picking the best cell from forty-eight is how spurious findings are made.

Contents

Source. WDFW Hatchery Escapement Reports — 27 annual PDFs plus the weekly in-season series. Parsed values were checked against every published statewide total in the annual reports; all 562 reconcile exactly.
Reading these numbers. Escapement here means fish returning to a hatchery rack or trap — not the total run to a river. Fish that spawn below the rack, are caught, or pass an idle trap are not counted. Weekly figures are preliminary in-season estimates; annual figures are final.
Joins. The 1998–99 and 1999–2000 reports use shorter facility names than later years; those are matched to the modern names where unambiguous, and names that the WDFW GIS layer resolves to one physical site are folded together so a hatchery's history is continuous. Egg-take goals are absent (NA) on many rows and are excluded from attainment rather than treated as zero.
Locations. Coordinates, county, WRIA and waterbody come from the WDFW HatcheryLikeFacilities GIS layer (WGS84), joined to the escapement records by facility name.
Known source quirks. The "trapped less released" (net) columns are published by WDFW as a separate calculation, not as a subtraction of the columns beside them — their own statewide totals fail that identity in about 30% of species-seasons. Those columns are carried through to the exports exactly as printed and are not used in any chart here. Every other column reconciles to WDFW's published statewide totals exactly.
